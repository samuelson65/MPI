FILTERS
Aggregates cover negotiated_type = 'negotiated' (fixed-dollar) rows only — percentage and derived rates use a different denominator and are excluded from price statistics. All computation runs in-browser via DuckDB WASM.

Connect a data source

Everything runs locally in your browser — no data leaves this machine.

How it's calculated

2025: $ per finding = recovered ÷ findings. Hit rate = findings ÷ audited.
2026 target = spend × target %.
Hit-rate decay: effective hit rate = expected hit rate × (2026 audits ÷ 2025 audits)−k. Auditing more means reaching lower-risk claims, so the hit rate falls; auditing fewer raises it.

Each claim is presented as a findings report: the values found on the claim, what each value means clinically, why the model treated it as supportive or concerning, and what to verify in the medical record.

Expected columns
  • Claim identifier — claim_number, claim_id, claimid
  • Model score — score, risk_score, probability
  • SHAP values — shap_drgcode, shap_losgm_ratio, shap_mcc_count …
  • Claim values — drgcode, losgm_ratio, providertaxid, discharge_status, mcc_count, cc_count, primary_diag, procedure_code

Column names are case-insensitive. SHAP columns are detected by the shap_ prefix.
